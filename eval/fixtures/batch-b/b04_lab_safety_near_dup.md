# Laboratory Safety & Equipment Guidelines (Synthetic 2026)

[SYNTHETIC TEST FIXTURE]

## 1. General Access

All students entering hardware or networking laboratories must wear valid ID badges, follow electrostatic discharge precautions, and record equipment checkout in the supervising lab engineer logbook before powering any rack or test bench.

## 2. Electrical Safety

Food and open beverages are strictly prohibited near laboratory workstations, power distribution units, and server racks. Damaged cables must be tagged out immediately.
